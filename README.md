**TYRE RETAIL SALES & INVENTORY ANALYSIS**

**PROJECT OVERVIEW**

This project analyzes tyre retail business data to understand overall sales performance, product performance, inventory levels, stock availability, and supplier operations. The analysis is performed using Microsoft Power BI to identify useful business insights and support data-driven decisions.
The project uses three datasets: Sales, Current Stock, and Supplier to analyse business performance and improve sales monitoring, inventory management, and supplier operations.

**BUSINESS QUESTIONS**

1. Which tyre brand generates the highest revenue?
2. Which product generates the highest sales?
3. What is the total number of units sold, and how much revenue does the business generate?
4. Which tyre brands have the highest and lowest stock quantities?
5. What is the total value of the current inventory?
6. Which supplier has the shortest and longest average delivery lead time?
7. Which suppliers have the most consistent delivery times, and which have greater variations?

**CHARTS** 

**Sales Analysis**

- Revenue by Brand: Compares total revenue generated by different tyre brands and identifies the highest-performing brands.
- Revenue Trend: Displays monthly revenue changes and helps identify high- and low-sales periods.
- Units Sold by Brand: Compares the quantity of tyres sold across different brands.
- Top 5 Products by Revenue: Highlights the five products generating the highest revenue.
- Sales & Return Overview: Shows the distribution of sales and return transactions and provides an overview of the return rate.
- Revenue by Price Level: Compares revenue generated across different product price categories.
  
**INVENTORY ANALYSIS**

- Stock Quantity by Brand: Compares the total stock quantities available across different tyre brands and identifies brands with the highest and lowest stock        levels.
- Top 5 Products by Stock Quantity: Highlights the five products with the highest available stock quantities.
- Inventory Value by Brand: Compares the total inventory value of different brands and identifies which brands hold the highest stock value.
- Number of Products by Last Restock Date: Shows the number of products restocked each month and helps understand restocking patterns.
 
**Supplier Analysis**

- Brand-Supplier Mapping: Shows the relationship between tyre brands and their respective suppliers.
- Average Lead Time by Supplier: Compares the average delivery lead time of different suppliers and identifies the fastest and slowest suppliers.
- Supplier Delivery Performance: Compares the minimum and maximum delivery lead times to understand supplier delivery performance.
- Lead Time Variation by Supplier: Shows the variation in delivery times across suppliers and helps identify suppliers with more consistent or inconsistent          delivery performance.

**SLICERS & FILTERS** 

- Brand: Filters the dashboard based on different tyre brands.
- Price Level: Filters products according to their price categories.
- Date Range: Filters sales data within a selected date range using start and end dates.
 These slicers allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyse specific business information.

**KPI CARDS**

- Total Revenue: Displays the overall revenue generated from tyre sales.
- Total Units Sold: Shows the total number of tyre units sold.
- Total Transactions: Displays the total number of cleaned transaction records.
- Total Sales Transactions: Shows the number of successful sales transactions.
- Total Return Transactions: Displays the total number of returned transactions.
- Return Rate: Shows the percentage of transactions recorded as returns.
- Total Products: Displays the total number of products available in the inventory.
- Total Stock: Shows the total quantity of products currently available.
- Total Inventory Value: Displays the overall monetary value of current stock.
- Average Stock per Product: Shows the average stock quantity available per product.
- Total Suppliers: Displays the total number of suppliers.
- Average Lead Time: Shows the average delivery time taken by suppliers.
- Minimum Lead Time: Displays the shortest recorded supplier delivery time.
- Maximum Lead Time: Displays the longest recorded supplier delivery time.
- Average Lead Time Variation: Shows the average variation in supplier delivery times
 
**KEY FINDINGS**
  
- The cleaned sales dataset contains 15,181 transaction records.
- Total revenue generated is approximately 896.08 million.
- A total of 862 return transactions were recorded.
- The overall return rate is approximately 5.68%.
- Brand-wise and product-wise analysis helps identify high- and low-performing products.
- Stock analysis provides insights into inventory distribution and product availability.
- Supplier analysis helps compare average delivery lead times and evaluate supplier efficiency.
  
**RECOMMENDATIONS**

- Focus on high-performing brands and identify opportunities to improve low-performing brands.
- Maintain suitable stock levels to avoid overstocking and product shortages.
- Investigate the reasons behind the 5.68% return rate and take steps to reduce returns.
- Work with suppliers to reduce delivery delays and ensure consistent stock availability.
- Use Power BI dashboards to monitor sales, inventory, and supplier performance for better business decisions.
